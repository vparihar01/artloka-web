# Design System Direction

## Experience attributes
Premium, warm, elegant, artistic, minimal, tactile and globally legible.

## Visual language
- Warm ivory backgrounds rather than clinical white.
- Earth, brass, patina, stone and muted botanical accents.
- Editorial serif display typography paired with a highly readable sans-serif body face.
- Large product imagery and calm spacing.
- Rounded elements used selectively; avoid generic software-dashboard styling.
- Indian heritage should appear through materials, craft, story and art direction—not ornamental clichés.

## Content hierarchy
1. Product discovery
2. Product confidence and specifications
3. Craftsmanship and provenance
4. Founder and brand narrative
5. Project and gifting enquiries

## Responsive targets
- 390px mobile
- 768px tablet
- 1280px desktop
- 1440px large desktop

## Accessibility baseline
- WCAG AA contrast targets
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus
- Semantic landmarks and headings
- Descriptive image alt text
- Form labels, errors and status messages
- Reduced-motion-safe transitions
