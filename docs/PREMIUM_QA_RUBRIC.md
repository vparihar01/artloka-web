# ArtLoka Premium QA Rubric

## Critical
- Adds on-site cart, checkout or payment language.
- Invents materials, certifications, dimensions, shipping timelines, scarcity, reviews or artisan stories.
- Uses placeholder imagery as final product representation.
- Shows raw Etsy tags or SEO keyword lists to customers.
- Breaks keyboard access, form validation, product routing or Etsy handoff.
- Causes horizontal scrolling at 390px, 768px, 1280px or 1440px.

## High
- Product is not the visual and informational hero of homepage or product page.
- Page feels like a generic ecommerce template, marketplace grid, SaaS dashboard or promotional landing page.
- Typography, spacing, image treatment or borders are inconsistent across the vertical slice.
- Mobile navigation or product decision flow feels secondary to desktop.
- Etsy CTA is unclear, overly aggressive or visually disconnected from the product decision.
- Required catalogue decision fields are missing from product pages without documented reason.
- Contrast or focus states fail practical accessibility review.

## Medium
- Copy is wordy, generic or uses repeated luxury clichés.
- Product card crops are inconsistent or hide important product shape.
- Specification grouping is technically correct but hard to scan.
- Related products are present but weakly contextual.
- Brand story overwhelms product discovery on the homepage.

## Low
- Minor spacing inconsistencies that do not affect comprehension.
- Non-blocking image crop preferences.
- Small copy refinements that improve tone but do not change claims.

## Review Checklist
- Product elevation: large real imagery, clear title, price, materials and dimensions.
- Editorial quality: quiet palette, fine borders, restrained motion and composed layout.
- Data integrity: every visible claim maps to catalogue or approved docs.
- Conversion clarity: Etsy purchase route and enquiry route are both clear.
- Accessibility: semantic landmarks, visible focus, labels, alt text and reduced-motion respect.
- Responsive quality: inspect 390px, 768px, 1280px and 1440px before completion.
