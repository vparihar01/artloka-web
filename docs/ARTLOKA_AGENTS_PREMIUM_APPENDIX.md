# ArtLoka Premium Experience Rules

Add this section to the repository-level `AGENTS.md` or reference it from that file.

## Premium brand objective
- The site must feel like a design-led, high-consideration interiors brand—not an Etsy mirror, mass-market catalogue or generic ecommerce template.
- Product, material, scale, craft and placement are the primary persuasion tools.
- Use quiet confidence, editorial restraint and precise product information.

## Art direction
- Warm ivory, parchment, stone, charcoal, deep oxblood/burgundy, antique brass and muted botanical accents.
- Editorial serif display typography plus a highly legible modern sans-serif body face.
- Large imagery, intentional crops, calm spacing, fine borders and minimal UI chrome.
- Avoid generic gradients, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, pill-heavy interfaces, dashboard styling, stock Indian motifs and decorative clichés.
- Avoid auto-playing carousels and unnecessary motion. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Copy rules
- No emojis, checkmark marketing lists, fake urgency, discount-first language or unsupported superlatives.
- Never use “affordable premium look”, “accessible to all”, “cheap” or “best price”.
- Never invent materials, process, artisan details, certifications, scarcity, reviews, delivery times, sustainability or workshop claims.
- Founder story supports trust but never overshadows the products and craft.

## Product-data presentation
- SEO Website Title is the public canonical product name.
- Original Etsy Title remains internal traceability data.
- Meta Description belongs in metadata, not duplicated visible copy.
- Website Description is the main editorial product story.
- Etsy URL powers the purchase CTA and outbound analytics.
- Etsy Tags and Website SEO Keywords support internal search, taxonomy and content planning; never render them as visible keyword lists.
- Show price, materials, dimensions, finishes, special features, room suitability and handmade note clearly.
- Show canopy/backplate and regional sockets only where relevant.
- Do not imply live inventory from finish options unless availability data supports it.

## Product imagery
- Use only approved real product images for product representation.
- Never use generated substitutes as factual product photographs.
- Require hero, detail and scale/lifestyle imagery before premium launch where applicable.
- Missing final imagery is a launch blocker, not permission to hide it with decorative UI.

## Visual QA
- Review affected routes at 390px, 768px, 1280px and 1440px.
- Inspect header, first viewport, image crops, mid-page transitions, conversion area and mobile navigation/filters.
- A page is not done only because tests pass; it must also meet the premium QA rubric and contain no accidental template styling.
