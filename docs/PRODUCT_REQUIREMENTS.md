# ArtLoka Product Requirements

## Product vision
Create the official ArtLoka digital home: a high-trust, visually rich catalogue and inspiration platform that turns search and social discovery into qualified Etsy visits and structured project enquiries.

## Positioning
**Heritage Craftsmanship. Styled for Modern Living.**

ArtLoka brings authentic Indian artistry into contemporary spaces through artisan-made and hand-finished décor and lighting designed for globally relevant interiors.

## Goals
1. Showcase the full approved product collection.
2. Help visitors discover products by product type, room, style, material and gifting occasion.
3. Build confidence through specifications, craftsmanship, founder narrative and clear Etsy purchase routing.
4. Generate organic discovery through search-friendly product and editorial pages.
5. Capture structured bulk, custom, gifting, trade and hospitality enquiries.
6. Measure which pages, collections and CTAs generate Etsy visits and enquiries.

## Non-goals for phase one
- On-site cart, checkout or payment capture.
- Live inventory synchronization with Etsy.
- Customer accounts, memberships or subscriptions.
- Instant consultation booking.
- Automatic publication of unreviewed Excel rows.

## Audiences
Priority order for year one:
1. Individual homeowners and décor enthusiasts.
2. Gift buyers.
3. Interior designers and architects.
4. Hospitality, corporate and bulk buyers.

Brand and conversion content:

- Trade and interior designers
- Hospitality projects
- Corporate and bulk gifting
- Custom enquiry
- Journal/inspiration

## Product page requirements
- Stable SEO URL and canonical metadata
- Image gallery with useful alt text
- Product title, SKU and category
- Materials, finishes and dimensions
- Electrical specification by region where relevant
- Craftsmanship and handmade note
- Room and styling suggestions
- Current Etsy CTA
- Customisation enquiry CTA
- Related products
- Product and breadcrumb structured data
- Claims-review protection for unresolved rows

## Trust principles
- Never overstate authenticity, materials, certifications or availability.
- Clearly separate product discovery on ArtLoka from checkout on Etsy.
- Explain natural material and hand-finished variation.
- Keep policy, shipping and final price confirmation on the Etsy listing unless verified directly for the website.
